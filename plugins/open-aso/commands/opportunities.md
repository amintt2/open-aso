Find the best opportunities for this app. Read-only: do not change anything.

$ARGUMENTS

1. `list_apps` to resolve the app (ask if it is ambiguous).
2. `get_app_keywords`: tracked keywords with a high opportunity score where the app ranks below 10 or not at all.
3. `get_keyword_suggestions`: new ideas with high opportunity.
4. If I named a keyword (or one stands out), `get_country_opportunities` to scan it across the largest storefronts.

Report three lists: quick wins (already ranking 11 to 50), new terms worth tracking, and countries worth localizing for. Each item gets one line on why. Popularity and downloads are modeled estimates; say so.
